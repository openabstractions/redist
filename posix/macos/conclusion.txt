Installed.

The three programs are in ~/.local/bin, which macOS does not put on PATH. Add
it once, in your shell profile:

    export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"

Deliberately not written for you: an installer that edits a shell profile is an
installer nobody can fully remove.

The background sweep runs at login and then every five minutes. Check it:

    launchctl print gui/$(id -u)/com.openabstractions.jobd

To remove everything this put on the machine, run exactly:

    ~/.local/share/abstraction/uninstall.sh

That leaves your job store (~/.abstraction) and your settings
(~/Library/Application Support/abstraction) alone; it prints the command that
removes those too.

This package was not signed and not notarised.
