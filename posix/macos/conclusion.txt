The shared-runtime LaunchAgent is registered for your graphical login session.

Programs are in ~/.local/bin. Add that directory to PATH in your shell profile
if desired. The installer leaves shell profiles unchanged.

    ~/.local/bin/openabstractions start
    ~/.local/bin/openabstractions status
    launchctl print gui/$(id -u)/com.openabstractions.jobd

Start activates the installed runtime and requires capability readiness. The
closed macOS Program-proof limitation remains; registration alone cannot make
those protected calls usable.

Remove installed programs and registration from your graphical session with:

    ~/.local/share/abstraction/uninstall.sh

Removal preserves accepted work and settings under your home directory.
Failed stop or deregistration verification retains the payload. A successful
bootout may include launchd forced shutdown; it is not a graceful-exit proof.

This package is unsigned and not notarised.
